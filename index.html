Who we are

Sardar Knitting deals in all kinds of cloth warp knitting, specializing in mesh fabrics and other high-quality fabrics. We use advanced warp knitting technology to make fabrics that offer comfort, durability and excellent performance.


Our tagline says it simply: quality knitting, lasting fabrics. We believe good fabric is the foundation of every good product, so we put precision into each roll.

Our mission

To supply fabrics that give manufacturers and makers the comfort, durability and performance they can build on.

Our commitment

Quality in every roll, clear communication on every order, and support for both custom orders and bulk supply.
